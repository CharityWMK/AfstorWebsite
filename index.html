Who we are

About Us

For millions of families across Sub-Saharan Africa, daily life still means gathering firewood, breathing in smoke, and cooking over an open flame. We believe it's time for that to change.

Afstor Solar Cooker harnesses the power of the sun to bring clean energy for cooking with zero CO2 emissions and zero compromise. It's more than a product. It's a leap forward, giving families instant access to the kind of energy independence the rest of the world already takes for granted.

Every Solar Cooker replaces the smoke and danger of an open fire with something safer, cleaner, and smarter. It even tracks its own impact, the hours of clean energy delivered and the CO2 emissions avoided, so every family can see, in real numbers, the difference they're making for their home and for the planet.

Our Solar Cooker is built to last and built to matter.

We're not just selling a product. We're working toward a future where no family has to choose between cooking dinner and protecting their health, where communities across Sub-Saharan Africa can thrive, breathe easier, and step confidently into a brighter, cleaner tomorrow.

Why it matters

How does Afstor solve the problem?

Afstor Solar Home System produces clean energy for cooking, lighting and appliances with 100% solar power and zero CO₂ emissions. It enables a family to leap into modern, sustainable energy use.

During five years of usage, one Afstor Solar Home System reduces CO₂ emissions equal to 260,000 km driven by car. You can track the usage hours of your purchased system and the resulting CO₂ compensation on a tracking website.
