Grow your business by bringing clean cooking home.

The opportunity

A product that sells on savings

Millions of families still cook with firewood and charcoal. Afstor gives them a cleaner option that pays for itself.

Easy to justify

At KES 4,000 a month on pay-as-you-go, the system costs less than a typical KES 1,000-a-week charcoal bill.

Backed by warranty

Every system carries a 2-year warranty on parts and labour, so customers buy with confidence.

Impact your customers can see

Each system tracks its own usage hours and CO₂ avoided, a story customers share with neighbours.

Partnership model

How we work together

Who we partner with

  • Solar and electronics dealers
  • Retail stores and agent networks
  • SACCOs and microfinance institutions offering asset finance
  • Implementation partners and NGO field teams

What partners receive

  • Product, sales and installation training
  • Technical backup from Afstor technicians for after-sales service
  • Marketing materials and demonstration units
  • Supply from our Nairobi assembly facility

Margins & commercial terms

Partner margins, minimum orders and pay-as-you-go collection arrangements are agreed per territory and partner type. We share full terms on our introductory call.

Pay-as-you-go made simple

Customers pay a KES 20,000 deposit, then KES 4,000 a month for 24 months, or KES 96,000 in full. Partners can sell either option.
