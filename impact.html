The impact of clean cooking

What changes when a kitchen goes solar

Health

Replacing an open fire removes smoke from the home, every meal, every day. Women and children, who spend the most time near the fire, benefit first. No flame also means fewer burns and fire risks.

Environment

Each system saves up to 4,000 kg of firewood a year, easing pressure on forests, and avoids about 6,400 kg of CO₂. Over five years, that equals the emissions of driving a car 260,000 km.

Household savings

Once installed, the sun is free. A family spending KES 1,000 a week on charcoal saves about KES 52,000 a year, and gets back the hours spent collecting fuel.
