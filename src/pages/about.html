Our story

It’s time for cooking to change

For millions of families across Sub-Saharan Africa, daily life still means gathering firewood, breathing in smoke and cooking over an open flame. We believe it’s time for that to change.

The Afstor Solar Cooker harnesses the power of the sun to bring clean energy for cooking with zero CO₂ emissions and zero compromise. It’s more than a product. It’s a leap forward, giving families instant access to the kind of energy independence the rest of the world already takes for granted.

Every Solar Cooker replaces the smoke and danger of an open fire with something safer, cleaner and smarter. It even tracks its own impact, the hours of clean energy delivered and the CO₂ emissions avoided, so every family can see, in real numbers, the difference they’re making for their home and for the planet.

Our Solar Cooker is built to last and built to matter.
