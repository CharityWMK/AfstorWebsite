Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you switch to solar cooking.

{{i:whatsapp}} Ask us on WhatsApp
Does it work on cloudy or rainy days?

Yes. The built-in battery stores solar energy, so you can keep cooking on cloudy or rainy days. On long stretches of bad weather, cooking may take a little longer.

Can I cook in the evening after the sun goes down?

Yes. The battery charges during the day, giving you up to 4 hours of cooking after sunset, enough for a typical family dinner.

How long does it take to cook a meal?

About the same as a charcoal jiko, and often faster, because there is no waiting for the charcoal to light. See What you can cook for times for popular meals.

How much will I save compared to charcoal or firewood?

Once installed, the sun is free, so you spend nothing on cooking fuel. A family spending KES 1,000 a week on charcoal saves about KES 52,000 a year.

How do I pay? Do you offer payment plans?

You can make a full cash payment of KES 96,000, or spread the cost with our pay-as-you-go plan: a deposit of KES 20,000, then KES 4,000 a month for 24 months.

Do you deliver and install it?

Yes. We deliver across Kenya, and our technicians install the system and show your family how to use it.

What warranty do I get, and where can I get repairs?

Every system comes with a 2-year warranty covering parts and labour. If anything goes wrong, call or WhatsApp our service team and a technician will come to you.

How do I clean and look after it?

Wipe the solar panels with a damp cloth every few weeks to remove dust, clean the cooking surface after use, and keep the unit in a dry place. That’s it.
