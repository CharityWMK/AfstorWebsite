The opportunity

A daily need, still met with smoke

For millions of families across Sub-Saharan Africa, cooking still means gathering firewood, breathing smoke and buying charcoal every week. Afstor replaces that recurring fuel spend with a solar asset the household owns.

“To be the leading provider of clean, affordable and intelligent off-grid cooking and distributed energy solutions across Africa and emerging markets.”

Afstor vision

Business model

Four routes to revenue and impact

{{i:home}}

Household sales & pay-as-you-go

KES 96,000 cash, or a KES 20,000 deposit plus KES 4,000 a month for 24 months (KES 116,000 total contract value). The monthly instalment is below a typical household charcoal bill.

{{i:school}}

Institutional & bulk

Multi-system installations for schools, clinics, churches and community groups, with bulk pricing, installation and maintenance.

{{i:store}}

Distribution partners

Dealers, SACCOs, microfinance institutions and implementation partners extend reach, finance and service beyond Nairobi.

{{i:leaf}}

Carbon & results-based finance

Metered CO₂ reductions from every unit create the data foundation for carbon-credit and results-based revenue.

Impact metrics & carbon-credit potential

Measured, not modelled

Every Afstor system tracks its own usage hours and CO₂ avoided, visible on a tracking website. This unit-level data supports transparent monitoring, reporting and verification for carbon and results-based finance.
