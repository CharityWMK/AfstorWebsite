The problem

Cooking shouldn’t cost a family its health.

For millions of families across Sub-Saharan Africa, daily life still means gathering firewood, breathing in smoke and cooking over an open flame. We believe it’s time for that to change.

  • {{i:wind}}Smoke in the kitchen. Open fires fill homes with smoke, every meal, every day.
  • {{i:clock}}Time and safety. Hours spent collecting wood, and the everyday danger of an open flame.
  • {{i:coins}}A weekly fuel bill. Charcoal and firewood drain household budgets and forests alike.

How Afstor solves it

The Afstor system produces clean energy for cooking with 100% solar power and zero CO₂ emissions. It even tracks its own impact, showing the hours of clean energy delivered and the CO₂ avoided, so every family can see the difference in real numbers.

How it works

Sunshine in. Hot meals out.

One integrated system that turns sunshine into clean, smoke-free cooking.

  1. 01 {{i:sun}}

    Capture

    High-efficiency 450W solar panels on the roof capture the sun’s energy all day.

  2. 02 {{i:battery}}

    Store

    A 48V LiFePO4 battery stores that energy, so you can cook on cloudy days and after dark.

  3. 03 {{i:cooktop}}

    Cook

    An efficient induction cooktop heats your pot directly: no flame, no smoke, up to 4 hours after sunset.
