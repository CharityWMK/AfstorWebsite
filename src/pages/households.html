Common questions

Before you buy

All FAQs {{i:arrow-right}}
Does it work on cloudy or rainy days?

Yes. The built-in battery stores solar energy, so you can keep cooking on cloudy or rainy days. On long stretches of bad weather, cooking may take a little longer.

How long does it take to cook a meal?

About the same as a charcoal jiko, and often faster, because there is no waiting for the charcoal to light.

Do you deliver and install it?

Yes. We deliver across Kenya, and our technicians install the system and show your family how to use it.

What warranty do I get, and where can I get repairs?

Every system comes with a 2-year warranty covering parts and labour. If anything goes wrong, call or WhatsApp our service team and a technician will come to you.
