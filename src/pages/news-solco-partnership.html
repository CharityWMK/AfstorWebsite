Afstor Energy Joins the SOLCO Partnership to Expand Solar Electric Cooking Across Last-Mile Communities

Afstor Energy has joined the SOLCO Partnership, committing to reach 5,000 households and pilot electric cooking in five institutions over the next 12 months.

{{i:calendar}} 25 November 2026 · 3 min read

Afstor Energy Limited has joined the SOLCO Partnership, a coalition working to bring electric cooking to last-mile communities across Africa.

Through the partnership, Afstor is committed to expanding access to solar-powered induction cooking in underserved and off-grid communities. Over the next 12 months, Afstor aims to reach 5,000 households and pilot electric cooking at five institutions, starting with schools.

Starting in Kenya, Afstor will work with government agencies, financial institutions, and local implementation and distribution partners to drive adoption through community demonstrations, consumer financing, installer training and after-sales support.

Key commitments

  • {{i:home}}5,000households in 12 months
  • {{i:school}}5institutional pilots, starting with schools
  • {{i:globe}}Kenya firstthen scaling across Africa

Afstor will also share lessons from its off-grid deployments and strengthen digital monitoring of cooker use and the emissions reductions it delivers. This data will help show how carbon finance and other funding mechanisms can make the cookers more affordable and support wider deployment.

“Partnering with organizations that share our vision for accessible, clean energy is key to scaling impact. By combining our solar induction technology with strong local distribution, we can bring clean cooking to more households and institutions.”

Afstor Energy

Through SOLCO, Afstor looks forward to connecting with partners who can help bring clean cooking to more households and institutions in last-mile communities across Africa.
