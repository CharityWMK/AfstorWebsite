Privacy Policy

How Afstor Energy Limited handles the personal data you share through this website, in line with Kenya’s Data Protection Act, 2019.

{{i:calendar}} Last updated 26 November 2026

Who we are

Afstor Energy Limited (“Afstor”, “we”) is the data controller for personal data collected through this website. You can reach us at 38988 Infinity Industrial Park, Nairobi, Kenya, by email at office@afstor.com or by phone on +254 181 100 200.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us through our enquiry forms:

  • your name and email address;
  • your phone number, if you provide it;
  • details about your enquiry, such as your county, organisation, role, the type of institution or business, and your message.

We do not ask for sensitive personal data, and we do not use this website to sell products or take payments.

Why we use it

We use your details only to respond to your enquiry: to answer questions, arrange demonstrations, prepare quotes, discuss partnerships or investment, and provide after-sales support. Our lawful basis is your consent, which you give by ticking the box on the form, and our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who sees it

Your enquiry is emailed to the Afstor team that handles it (sales, partnerships, or investor and government relations). Our website host and email provider process it on our behalf to deliver the message. We share it with no one else unless the law requires us to.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for up to 24 months after our last contact with you, then delete them, unless we need them longer for an ongoing order, contract or legal obligation. The website does not store form submissions in a database. To protect against abuse it keeps short-lived security records that contain no names, emails or IP addresses, which are deleted after 90 days.

Cookies, analytics and third-party content

The website sets one strictly necessary cookie, afstor_form, to protect our forms from misuse. It is deleted when you close your browser and does not need your consent.

Google Analytics (only with your consent). If you click “Accept” in our cookie banner, we use Google Analytics 4, provided by Google, to understand how the website is used so we can improve it. It records the pages you visit, how you arrived (for example from a search engine), your approximate location (country and city, derived from your IP address, which Google Analytics does not store), your device and browser type, and actions such as choosing a pathway, clicking a phone, email or WhatsApp link, or successfully sending an enquiry. It never receives what you type into our forms: no names, email addresses, phone numbers or messages. It sets the cookies _ga and _ga_<ID>, which last up to 13 months. We have switched off Google signals and advertising features, and we keep analytics data for no longer than 14 months. Google may process this data outside Kenya under its own safeguards.

Your choice. Analytics stays off unless you accept. You can change your mind at any time using Cookie settings (also in the footer of every page); declining removes the analytics cookies. You can also block Google Analytics on all websites with Google’s browser add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Embedded content. Some pages show content from other services, which may set their own cookies or receive your IP address when that content loads: Google Fonts (typefaces), YouTube (videos), LinkedIn (embedded posts) and Google Maps (our location). Cloudflare Turnstile may also check that form submissions come from a person rather than a bot.

Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 you have the right to be told how your data is used; to access it; to ask us to correct inaccurate data or delete data we no longer need; to object to or restrict its use; to withdraw consent at any time; and to receive your data in a portable format. To use any of these rights, email office@afstor.com. We will respond within the time the law allows.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) in Kenya.

Keeping it secure

The website uses encrypted HTTPS connections, validates every form submission on our server, and limits who at Afstor can read enquiries. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you and the ODPC of any breach as the law requires.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
